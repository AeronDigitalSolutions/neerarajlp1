About Neera Raj

Where Artistry Meets Elegance

Neera Raj is a celebrated bridal makeup artist with a passion for enhancing every bride's natural beauty. With over 8 years of expertise in the art of bridal transformation, she has crafted stunning looks for hundreds of brides across India.

Specializing in everything from classic traditional bridal looks to contemporary glam, Neera uses only premium international products to ensure your makeup stays flawless from the ceremony to the reception and beyond.
